faq

the questions the herd keeps asking.

what is a seat

a membership on moo market. there are exactly 1,000, each one a cow. a seat carries its own wCOIN bag (milk lands in it), a self-set price, a b20 identity, gets milked every epoch, trades on the herd’s own barn, and will be leasable. it is not equity and not a promise of profit.

what is $MOO

$MOO is the market token on base — a normal erc-20 you can buy and sell on uniswap.

where does my milk actually go

into your seat’s own bag, not your wallet. an unattended seat compounds itself. taking free milk out takes 24 hours, and selling the seat cancels a withdrawal in flight, so a buyer always gets the bag they were shown.

can i take the seed back out

no, and that is the point. the seed is permanent seat capital: it lives in your seat’s bag forever and you realize it by selling the seat, because a buyer pays for the seat including its bag. if it were refundable, seeding would be free in every real sense and every bag would be empty within a day.

what is the milk

each epoch the milkhouse writes covered calls on the pooled wCOIN and splits the premium across all 1,000 seats. some epochs pay more, some pay less, some can pay zero. no APY promises. the milk is what the milk is.

can i lose money

yes. seat prices can fall, milk can be small or zero, contracts can fail, and wCOIN carries wrapper risk. read the risk section in the docs before you trade.
